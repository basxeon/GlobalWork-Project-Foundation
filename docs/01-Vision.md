# GlobalWork OS — Vision

## Purpose

GlobalWork OS is an internal operations system for visa and work-permit agencies. Its stated V1 goal is to replace a workflow centred on OneDrive, PDF readers, Word, and Excel with one browser-based workspace.

## Confirmed product goals

- Reduce repetitive staff work.
- Keep case information in one place.
- Support collaboration for 2–3 staff members; the updated architecture also targets SMEs of 2–10 users.
- Provide a foundation for a future vertical SaaS, without building SaaS features first.

## V1 outcomes

V1 should enable a staff member to manage a case, its documents, tasks, and timeline from the system. The dashboard is the starting point for daily work.

## Scope boundaries

Included in MVP: authentication, companies, applicants, cases, documents, tasks, and dashboard reporting.

Deferred: customer portal, multi-company/multi-tenancy, billing, analytics, workflow engine, AI copilot, and advanced notifications/calendar capabilities.

## Decisions required

- Define the business's actual case types and case lifecycle.
- Define user roles and access rules.
- Confirm which document types and government forms are in scope for V1.
- Confirm the initial storage provider and deployment environment.
